Humanoids and robot dogs with a US price: a first look.

Humanoid robots and robot dogs with a price on a US seller’s own listing, read on 13 November 2026. Sorted by price, nothing else. We have not used them.

Pre-order only

These can be ordered but not yet delivered, so they are not in the table.

  • Unitree R1 Basic (humanoid): a pre-order at Toborlife, listed at $8,990, “8-Month Warranty”. The store lists it in stock, but its own text says: “Be among the very first to receive the Unitree R1 when shipments begin in the coming months.” So we list it as a pre-order.
  • 1X NEO (humanoid): ordered from 1X, on its own US order page: “$20,000” for “Ownership with 3-Year Warranty”, or “$499/mo”; “$200 Deposit Due Today”, “Fully Refundable”; “US Deliveries start 2026”.
